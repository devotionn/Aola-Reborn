# v0.8 — Mist Investigation & Battle Items

v0.8 closes the third investigation loop and turns recovery resources into a coherent party/battle item system.

## Goals

1. Complete Mist Mirror Wetland as a real investigation chapter rather than a world-only prelude.
2. Prevent player combat from deadlocking when all regular move PP reaches zero.
3. Reuse one item rule layer across exploration bag and battle.
4. Let exploration items target any party member instead of always targeting the leader.
5. Give the two Ember Moss Grove exclusives real growth payoffs.

## Third investigation — Mist Mirror Wetland

The third region now contains a complete persistent quest line:

1. meet **Observer Xiyao / 观测员 · 汐遥**
2. accept the wetland investigation
3. record the central Mist Mirror beacon
4. trace the phase delay to the northeast Mirror Nest
5. challenge the original guardian species **镜雾鹭 / mistMirrorHeron**
6. return to Xiyao and submit the report
7. receive the third-investigation reward

Persistent flags:

- `mistQuestAccepted`
- `mistBeaconSurveyed`
- `mistGuardianDefeated`
- `mistQuestRewarded`

### v0.7 save compatibility

v0.7 already allowed players to survey the beacon before the complete quest existed. v0.8 intentionally honors that existing `mistBeaconSurveyed` flag. Returning players can accept the investigation and continue from the Mirror Nest instead of repeating the survey.

### Third guardian encounter

New original species:

- **镜雾鹭 / mistMirrorHeron**
- water element
- high speed / spirit profile
- special encounter only in the current slice
- cannot be captured during the investigation challenge

Battle presentation is region-specific:

- title: `雾镜湿地 · 镜池雾巢`
- subtitle: `雾幕守望 · 镜雾鹭`

Quest completion reward:

- 680 credits
- Starshine Tonic ×2
- Star Energy Refill ×2

The guardian battle itself also grants its configured combat reward before the report reward.

## Party-targeted exploration inventory

`BagScene` no longer assumes that every recovery item targets party slot 0.

The player can now:

- select any current party member by clicking its card
- switch target with left/right or A/D
- use a Starshine Tonic on the selected member
- use a Star Energy Refill on the selected member
- inspect the selected member's HP, condition and four current PP values

Normal Starshine Tonics do **not** revive a creature at 0 HP. This keeps the fainted state meaningful and leaves room for an explicit revival item later.

Core item functions now accept a creature UID:

- `useTonicOnCreature(save, creatureUid)`
- `usePpRefillOnCreature(save, creatureUid)`

Leader wrappers remain available for field shortcuts and existing callers.

## In-battle item menu

Battle now has a formal item menu:

- `I` opens battle items
- Starshine Tonic: restores HP and clears conditions
- Star Energy Refill: restores roughly 50% PP across the active creature's four moves
- Capture Capsule: routes into the existing capture flow when capture is allowed
- `H` remains the fast tonic shortcut

Recovery / PP items consume the player's action opportunity: after successful use, the opponent acts before the round resolves.

The exploration bag can target any party member; the v0.8 battle menu intentionally targets the currently active creature only.

## Player PP exhaustion fallback

v0.7 already prevented NPCs from reusing zero-PP moves forever. v0.8 adds the same deadlock protection to the player side.

New rule helpers:

- `allMovePpDepleted(creature)`
- `choosePlayerMove(creature, slot)`

If at least one normal move still has PP, selecting an exhausted move remains invalid and the player must choose another move, switch creature, or restore PP.

If **all four** regular moves reach zero PP, the player receives the emergency action:

- **余辉震荡 / strugglePulse**

The battle UI explicitly exposes that emergency state instead of leaving four unusable buttons.

## Known gaps

- battle item menu targets the active creature only; party-target selection in battle is not implemented yet
- no revival item exists yet
- only `scorch` and `sluggish` conditions exist
- no stat-stage, weather, field, physical/special/status move categories, accuracy stages or critical-hit model yet
- the three regions still use placeholder geometric rendering rather than production tilesets
- creature/NPC art, battle backgrounds, skill VFX, animation and audio are still placeholder or absent
- no Playwright browser-flow coverage yet
- Phaser remains in the large main client bundle and still triggers the bundle-size warning

## Recommended v0.9 direction

v0.9 should stop expanding world breadth temporarily and focus on presentation and production-readiness:

1. original visual identity and public-facing game name
2. production tileset / map rendering pipeline for Starport + three regions
3. first original creature illustration batch and NPC portraits
4. battle backgrounds, entrance animation and elemental skill VFX
5. audio manager + original UI/battle ambience hooks
6. Playwright smoke flow for starter -> first quest -> battle -> capture -> bag
7. bundle splitting / lazy scene loading
8. save schema migration versioning before further persistent-system expansion

## IP boundary

`Aola` remains a development repository codename. Public/commercial distribution should use an original product name and original art, maps, music, character design, story text and UI assets.
